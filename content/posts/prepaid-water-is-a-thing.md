---
title: "I Discovered Prepaid Water is a Thing"
date: 2026-04-23
---

You know how you can buy prepaid electricity for your house? You buy a code, punch it in, and you've got power. Simple.

Well, it turns out you can do the exact same thing for water. I stumbled across this today and it's kind of blown my mind.

It's all thanks to something called the Standard Transfer Specification, or STS. It's a system that was actually born right here in South Africa for prepaid electricity. It's so successful it's become a global standard. The genius part is that they're using the *exact same system* for water.

So, you go to the same shop where you buy your electricity voucher, but you ask for a water voucher instead. You get a 20-digit code, punch it into a little keypad in your house, and your water meter gets topped up. When the credit runs out, a valve closes, and the water stops until you top it up again.

It's a really simple, elegant solution to a complex problem. You don't need complex smart meters with constant internet connections for every house. You just need a meter with a valve and a keypad, using a vending system that already exists everywhere.

It makes me wonder what else could use a simple, offline-first system like this. It's a great reminder that sometimes the most robust solutions aren't the most high-tech ones.
